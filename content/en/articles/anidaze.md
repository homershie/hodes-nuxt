## I. The Pain of Fragmented Anime Tracking

As a visual designer and avid anime fan, I found that "tracking anime" was surprisingly inefficient in terms of information access. Every new season, I'd repeat a mechanical loop: check air times on site A, scores on tab B, records on platform C.

This **information fragmentation** didn't just interrupt the viewing experience—as a designer it felt wrong. Information should be elegant and intuitive. So I built [AniDaze Anime Calendar](http://www.anidaze.com) with a simple goal: **an anime tracking tool that combines beauty and function.**

## II. Design Philosophy: Intuitive Aesthetics at a Glance

For UI/UX, AniDaze's core idea is "subtraction." We don't need noise—we need the right information at the right moment.

I designed four view modes (weekly and monthly, each with two layouts). This isn't just layout variation—it's different interpretations of **"time flow."** You can plan your weekend watchlist like a calendar, or quickly scan the season's trending shows.

## III. The Power of Integration: Goodbye Tab Hopping

This was my biggest investment. AniDaze integrates AniList, Bangumi, Wikipedia and more. Through technical means, I unified scores, air times, and multi-language metadata that were scattered across platforms.

For users, this means: **no more "tab hopper" workflow.** On one page you can compare ratings, decide if a show is worth your time, and update your watch status—all in one place.

## IV. Responsive Experience: A Designer's Pride

AniDaze doesn't have a native app yet, but I have high standards for the web version's **RWD (Responsive Web Design)**.

I especially optimized mobile controls—e.g., an intuitive **Bottom Sheet** for filters, search, and tracking with one hand. For me, the web version shouldn't be a compromise on mobile; it should feel like a perfectly adapted experience.

## V. Conclusion: An Evolving Side Project

For me, AniDaze isn't just a playground for Next.js and Supabase—it's a record of "solving problems." The web version now supports following shows, tracking watch status, and rating with notes.

If you're looking for a clean, efficient, design-focused anime tracking tool, check out AniDaze. No ad clutter—just a space built for anime fans.
